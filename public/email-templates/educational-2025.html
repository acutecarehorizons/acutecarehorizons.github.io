📋 Case Study 1: Acute Chest Pain +
A 45-year-old male presents to the ED with acute chest pain. What's your first step?
A) Order a chest X-ray
B) Perform an ECG immediately
C) Administer aspirin
D) All of the above
Correct Answer: D
Our acute care protocols provide step-by-step guidance for chest pain evaluation, including immediate ECG, aspirin administration, and appropriate imaging based on clinical presentation.
👶 Case Study 2: Pediatric Fever +
A 2-year-old presents with fever of 103°F. What's your assessment priority?
A) Check for rash
B) Assess mental status
C) Look for signs of dehydration
D) All of the above
Correct Answer: D
Our pediatric protocols emphasize comprehensive assessment including mental status, hydration status, and rash evaluation for febrile children.
